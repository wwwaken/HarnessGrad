Why the sub-requirement qualifies (the scope is enumerated, not asserted)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The v4 run was a LOCAL pilot of this platform: its directory is
  `/tmp/harnessgrad-runs/hotpotqa-h0-10pct-v4`, under this machine's own run-ID convention. No
  upstream or third-party publication can hold its receipts, so "search the internet" is not a
  remaining avenue.
* The receipts EXISTED: the historical `ls` listing in
  `~/.codex/sessions/2026/10/12/rollout-2026-10-12T13-14-44-01a12e88-...jsonl:16` prints
  `hotpotqa-10pct-v4-split.json`, `hotpotqa-h0-10pct-v4-console.log` and
  `hotpotqa-h0-10pct-v4-ledger.json` (1,179 bytes), and line 85 of the other transcript captures
  `qa-h0-incomplete-index.json records [('hotpotqa', 'hotpotqa-h0-10pct-v4', 'incomplete',
  '/tmp/harnessgrad-runs/hotpotqa-h0-10pct-v4'), ...]`.
* They are absent over the enumerated on-machine scope: both `/tmp/codex` and `/tmp/harnessgrad-runs`
  are gone; `HarnessGrad-evidence-archive/` holds only `chess/`, `codex/` and `qwen-baseline/`; the
  two stranded pre-rewrite clones hold adapter source only; and `w-hotpotrec` already searched the
  repository object store, history, reflog, stashes, `runs/`, `runs_discarded_*`, `tests/fixtures/`,
  the named archives, `/mnt/20t/xubuqiang/hg-census/` and 722 session transcripts.
* The deletion cause is NOT established and is NOT attributed. The recorded `/tmp` hygiene patterns
  (`/tmp/hg-task-*`, `/tmp/harnessgrad-pytest-*`, the `hg-w*` scratch sets in
  `/mnt/20t/xubuqiang/hg-census/tmp_cleanup.py:55-64`) do not cover `/tmp/harnessgrad-runs` or
  `/tmp/codex`.
* The wording therefore differs from the `bird_minidev` / `ds1000` precedent on purpose: those say
  "not present in this repository and is not published upstream"; saying that here would overstate,
  because the receipts existed on this machine and were lost.
