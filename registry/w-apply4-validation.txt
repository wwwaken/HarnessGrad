The worker's drafts were strengthened before applying
----------------------------------------------------

Reading them showed the same pattern seen with w-fevconv and w-hotpotrec: the rewrites are
semantically close but drop *specific* constraints. The parent therefore appended to each replacement
the constraints that were lexically dropped and still load-bearing:

* the explicit fixture path of the bounded first-five custody record
  (`tests/fixtures/hg_wsel/<dataset>.json`);

  converter revision or runtime execution must not be inferred;

* for physicsquestions, sportsunderstanding and strategyqa, that actual pristine execution applies
  only to a future explicit pristine opt-in with the retained export/private-input and ownership
  boundary;
* for spreadsheetbench, that selected=5 is not eligibility or executed coverage and that the withdrawn
  `scorer-offline-closure` / `artifact-only-state-export` gates do not condition closure or admission,
  with their limits and opt-in boundary retained.

What this does NOT do
---------------------
- It closes nothing. All four blockers stay open with every dataset-specific obligation intact
  (row-level gradability for spreadsheetbench, original-benchmark scorer/split for strategyqa under an
  explicit `undetermined`, and so on).
- It records no measured score. Every discriminating example is a constructed synthetic
  (target, response) pair substituted into readable source; no scorer, adapters or dataset row was
  executed.
- It does not claim the retained upstream excerpts were re-authenticated against the live upstream.
- It edits no historical record, frozen receipt or run ID, and reinstates no withdrawn shared gate.
