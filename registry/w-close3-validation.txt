Decision
--------
The reading is the one the operator fixed on 2026-10-15 and which is recorded in the registry's own
`review.notes`: a `dataset-journal-gap` is discharged when the journal coverage its own `next_action`
names exists, and the diagnostic-time clauses are preconditions on a future, separately authorized
diagnostic rather than conditions on closure. The earlier note deliberately left `reasoning_gym` open for
two named, not-merely-diagnostic-time obligations - "an original generation receipt plus behavioural
eligibility" - and both are now discharged.
