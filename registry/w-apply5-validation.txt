What this does NOT do
---------------------
- It closes nothing: `appworld / successful-task-evidence` stays open and is satisfied only by a
  genuine successful measured actor task.
- It records no new measurement, no execution and no budget change; no run, model server, endpoint,
  GPU or scorer is touched.
- It does not edit a frozen receipt or run ID, and does not reclassify any retained loss as invalid or
  unmeasured on speculation - the review says so and this integration preserves it.
