It does not select a verifier-best sample for test@N, estimate pass@k for k>1, or require k successes for pass^k. For n sampled attempts with c successes, mean c/n estimates pass@1; success-at-least-once and all-k-success are different statistics. At k=1 some binary definitions coincide, but protocol/split/budget still matter. The handoff explicitly maps the six repeats to GSM8K, MuSiQue, 2Wiki, τ-bench, Reasoning Gym and BIRD (`registry/w-evidence-external-sources.md:1317`).
